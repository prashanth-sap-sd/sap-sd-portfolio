# Cutover Checklist (SAP SD/OTC)

## Pre-Cutover
- Confirm scope freeze and transport plan
- Validate master data readiness (customer/material/pricing)
- Confirm open order migration approach (if applicable)
- Testing sign-offs (SIT/UAT) and known issues list
- Output readiness (forms/EDI) checks

## Cutover Execution
- Execute agreed sequence of steps
- Validate sample transactions end-to-end (order → delivery → billing)
- Reconciliation checks with FI touchpoints (high level)

## Post-Go-Live / Hypercare
- Monitor critical queues/issues
- Daily defect triage cadence
- Stabilization checklist and handover plan
