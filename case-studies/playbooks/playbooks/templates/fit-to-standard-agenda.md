# Fit-to-Standard Workshop Agenda (SAP SD/OTC)

## Objectives
- Confirm TO-BE OTC flow using SAP best practices
- Identify gaps and decisions needed for design

## Attendees
Business owner, SD lead, FI/MM reps, testing lead, key users

## Agenda (90–120 mins)
1. Current process overview (AS-IS)
2. Walk through standard OTC in SAP (TO-BE)
3. Decisions:
   - order types, pricing rules, delivery/billing flow
4. Gaps & RICEFW needs (if any)
5. Testing and cutover considerations
6. Action items + owners
