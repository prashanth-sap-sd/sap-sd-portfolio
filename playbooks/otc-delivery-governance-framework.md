# OTC Delivery Governance Framework (Senior Consultant Model)

## Objective

Provide structured ownership of SD workstream from design through hypercare.

---

## Phase 1 — Discovery & Fit-to-Standard

- Conduct OTC process walkthrough
- Identify customization vs. standard
- Document RICEFW gaps
- Align pricing & billing strategy early

---

## Phase 2 — Configuration & Validation

- Configure sales document flow
- Validate item category & schedule line determination
- Execute pricing matrix validation
- Align delivery & billing copy control

---

## Phase 3 — Integration Testing

- SD–MM ATP validation
- SD–FI account determination testing
- Credit management validation
- Regression test across scenarios

---

## Phase 4 — Cutover & Hypercare

- Pre-go-live checklist validation
- Billing flow confirmation
- Daily defect triage model
- Stabilization documentation

---

## Senior Consultant Principle

Ownership does not stop at configuration.  
True delivery responsibility includes stabilization and governance alignment.
