# OTC Delivery Governance Framework (Senior Consultant Model)

## Objective

Provide structured ownership of SD workstream from design through hypercare.

---

## Phase 1 — Discovery & Fit-to-Standard

- Conduct OTC process walkthrough
- Identify customization vs. standard
- Document RICEFW gaps
- Align pricing & billing strategy early

---

## Phase 2 — Configuration & Validation

- Configure sales document flow
- Validate item category & schedule line determination
- Execute pricing matrix validation
- Align delivery & billing copy control

---

## Phase 3 — Integration Testing

- SD–MM ATP validation
- SD–FI account determination testing
- Credit management validation
- Regression test across scenarios

---

## Phase 4 — Cutover & Hypercare

- Pre-go-live checklist validation
- Billing flow confirmation
- Daily defect triage model
- Stabilization documentation

---

## Senior Consultant Principle

Ownership does not stop at configuration.  
True delivery responsibility includes stabilization and governance alignment.
# OTC Delivery Governance Framework (Playbook)

## Purpose
A reusable playbook for delivering stable OTC outcomes across implementations, enhancements, and production support.

**Experience signals:** 10+ years • 3 full life-cycle implementations • Delivered across 5 ASAP phases

---

## OTC Scope
- Inquiry/quotation → sales order → delivery → PGI → billing → FI posting
- LE: shipping, picking, packing, routes, transportation, shipments

---

## Delivery Standards
### Requirements to Build
- Confirm process variants: rush/cash/standard, intercompany, third-party
- Define master data readiness (customer/material/conditions)
- Identify integration touchpoints (FI/MM/WM/PP)

### Test Strategy
- Unit → SIT → Regression → UAT readiness
- Defect triage rules: severity, business impact, ownership, retest gates

### Go-Live Readiness
- Cutover checklist, transport plan, fallback plan
- Hypercare runbook: triage → fix → validate → prevent recurrence

---

## What “good” looks like
- Minimal critical issues at go-live
- Clean documentation so support teams can operate confidently
- Clear ownership and communication across stakeholders
